## 🏛️ Event Overview
The new project **"Silence Escape"** is finally approaching! ✨
Escape from the Ancient City by shaking off the tracking of the "Cops" approaching from behind and the Warden reacting to sound.

## ⚖️ Basic Rules for Fairness
To ensure all participants can enjoy equally, we will strictly enforce the following rules:

* **Unified Equipment:** To eliminate equipment differences, only equipment distributed by the management will be used. Bringing in any items is strictly prohibited. ⚔️
* **Strict Adherence to Terms of Service:** Please be sure to check the [Official Terms of Service](https://www.kamosaba.net/tos).
* **Prohibited Actions:** Exploiting bugs, cheating, using external tools, or slandering other players will result in immediate disqualification and punishment. 🚫

## 📝 How to Join
No special pre-registration is required. On the day, please join by following these steps:

1. **Connect to the Server:** Log in to the Kamosaba main server as usual. 💻
2. **Follow Staff Instructions:** Staff will begin guidance from 14:00. Follow instructions to move to the dedicated event venue. 🚩
3. **Capacity:** We assume a capacity of the **first 20 people**.

## ⚠️ Requirements & Penalty Regulations
For smooth operation, we have set the following conditions:

* **Minimum Participants:** The event will start with **5 or more** players.
    * If there are fewer than 5 people, the event will be postponed, and you will wait on the survival server.
* **Prohibitions and Punishments on Event Servers:**
    * If you are banned on an event server due to rule violations, you will be **permanently disqualified** from participating in all future event servers. ⚠️
    * However, you can continue to use the regular survival server.

## 📅 Date, Time & Audience
* **Date & Time:** Sunday, February 8th, 14:00~
* **Target Audience:** 2nd-year junior high school students (approx. age 13-14) or older.

A situation of extreme limits where "it's over if you make a sound." Use your stealth skills and teamwork to succeed in escaping from the Ancient City! 🙇‍♂️✨
