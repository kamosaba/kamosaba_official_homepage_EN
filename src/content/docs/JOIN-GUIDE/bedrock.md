# 📱 Bedrock Edition Join Guide

Explaining how to join "Kamosaba" from Bedrock Edition (Switch, Mobile, PS4/5, Xbox, Win10, etc.). ✨

### 📝 Steps to Join

1. **Open the Friends Screen**
   Launch Minecraft and select the "Play" -> "Friends" tab.

2. **Add a Friend**
   Press the "Add Friend" button, search for the following gamertag, and send a request.
   * **Gamertag**: `kamosaba`

3. **Join the Server**
   Once the friend registration is complete, the server will appear in your friends list, and you will be able to join. 🦆

### ⚠️ Precautions and Troubleshooting

* **If you cannot send a friend request**
* **If you cannot enter because the number of people is at its limit (MAX)**

If problems like the above occur, please do not leave them unaddressed and **contact the Official Discord immediately**. Management staff will assist you. 📩

* **Official Discord**: [https://discord.gg/xRDgzwAhM2](https://discord.gg/xRDgzwAhM2)
